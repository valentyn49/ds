## Overview

The footer closes every page of the product. The big footer holds the Socrates logo, the legal notice, three columns of links and a legal bar with the copyright and legal links. The small footer is a single centered line with the copyright text. Both span the full width of the page, with their content inset by a horizontal margin.

## Behaviors

The footer is a `footer` landmark. Its two groups of links are `nav` regions labelled "Footer" and "Legal", and each link group has a heading that labels it. Every item is a real link.

- **Keyboard:** Tab moves through the links in reading order: the link columns left to right, then the legal links.
- **Focus:** Figma defines no link focus look, so a focused link shows the existing `shadow-focus-primary` ring.
- **Hover and pressed:** Figma defines no look for them, so links do not change on hover.
- **Destinations:** Figma gives no link destinations. The links use `#` as a placeholder, and the component prevents it from navigating. This is unresolved and needs the product's real URLs.
- **No animation or other interaction** is defined.
