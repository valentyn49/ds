## Overview

An accordion reveals or hides content inside an expandable section. The Figma **collapse** component is one independent item: a header with a title and a chevron, and a content area that shows when the item is open. The header can also show a left icon, a subtitle, a link and a Button. It has **2 states** and one size.

## Behaviors

Figma defines a single item that is either closed or open. It does not define a group, so each item opens and closes on its own and opening one does not close another.

- **Click the header:** toggles the item. The whole header row, padding included, is the target.
- **Enter or Space** on the focused header: toggles the item.
- **Chevron:** points down when closed and up when open, and always matches the state.
- **Content:** shown only when the item is open.
- **Tab:** the header is focusable. A Button or Link in the header are separate controls with their own focus stops, and clicking them does not toggle the item.
- **Semantics:** the header is a native `button` with `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` points to the content panel. The panel is a `region` labelled by the title.

Figma does not specify a focus appearance. To keep keyboard use visible, a focused header shows the existing `shadow-focus-primary` ring.

Nested accordions, animation, exclusive expansion and extra header actions are not defined in Figma and are not built.
