## Overview

A text input lets a person enter a single line of text. The Figma **input** component is a stack of an optional label, the field and an optional footnote. The field can show a left icon and a right icon, and the label can show an info icon. It has **7 states** and one size.

## Formatting and content

- The label sits above the field, 8px apart. The info icon follows the label text with an 8px gap.
- The footnote sits below the field, 8px apart. Figma has one footnote text property, used in every state. In the `valid` and `error` states the footnote color matches the field border (green/700 and alert/600).
- Icons sit inside the field, 8px from the text, 16px from the field edge.
- Placeholder text is gray/500. Entered text is gray/900. In the `disabled` state the text is gray/400 and the field fill is gray/100.
